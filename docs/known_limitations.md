# Known Limitations (Internal Beta)

- Sample CIRSOC shape data is manually curated and incomplete.
- Sample materials are demonstration records, not an official material library.
- Sample rails are fake test data only.
- No official CIRSOC/CISC/AISC compliance checks yet.
- No fatigue checks.
- No torsional/warping stress checks.
- No LTB checks.
- No rail local stress or wheel-patch checks.
- No weld strength checks.
- No load combinations beyond the current simplified workflow.
- No moving lateral envelope support yet.
- No UI.
- No PDF/DOCX export.
- Engineering review and independent verification are required before project use.
